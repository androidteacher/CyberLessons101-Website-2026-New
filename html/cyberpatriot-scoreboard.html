Track Your Teams in Real Time!

CyberPatriot Competition Weekend Score Tracker

This self-hosted tracker scrapes the official CyberPatriot scores for the teams you follow and brings them together on one clean, live scoreboard — perfect for watching your whole program compete on competition weekends.

Teams are grouped by division (Platinum, Gold, Silver), sortable by any round, and refreshed automatically. Put it up on a classroom display or projector, or share the link with students, parents, and administrators.

Live & auto-refreshing

Scores pull straight from the official site.

Grouped by division

Platinum, Gold, and Silver on one board.

Sort by any round

Click a column to re-rank instantly.

Just enter your team numbers!

Add the teams you follow and the board does the rest.

Built for CyberPatriot coaches who want to follow their teams at a glance.

Run the Scoreboard

Copy and paste this command to launch the tracker, then open http://localhost in your browser.

docker run -d -p 80:80 --restart always --name cyberpatriot-scoreboard joshbeck2024/cyberpatriot-scoreboard-scraper-2026

Requires Docker. Maps host port 80 → container port 80.
